## At a glance

Bump is a technology consulting company founded by two entrepreneurs in Montréal, Québec. Our mission is to help SMEs become more efficient, intelligent and secure through solutions suited to their needs. Our three divisions cover workflows, AI and software, and cybersecurity.

## Technology should serve the work.

A useful system helps a team complete a task, understand a situation or make a decision. We approach automation, software development and security from that perspective: start with operations, then choose the technical means.

We want to connect what already works and build what is missing within a clear scope. A new application or AI agent is not an end in itself. Its role should be understandable to the people using it.

## A Québec starting point.

Our initial clients are Québec SMEs. Montréal is our base, with a bilingual identity and presentation designed to support international growth.

Needs differ from one business to another. Each conversation focuses on the processes, tools, constraints and goals of the engagement rather than a solution applied identically to everyone.

## Three complementary areas of expertise.

Our business model follows audit, analysis, proposal, implementation and maintenance. Deliverables, responsibilities and follow-up are defined within each project.

- Workflow Optimization: analyse processes, automate repetition and connect tools.
- AI Integration & Software Development: develop applications and integrate AI into defined use cases.
- Cybersecurity: carry out authorized audits, address weaknesses and organize follow-up.

## A vision to build, not a promise already delivered.

In the long term, we aim to develop our own specialized systems and agents, including Cyber AI, Developer AI and Workflow AI. This infrastructure represents our future vision.

These systems are not presented as available products. The site distinguishes that ambition from our current consulting, development and integration services. Animations explain those services using fictional scenarios.
