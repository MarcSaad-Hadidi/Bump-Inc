## At a glance

Bump is a technology consulting company founded by two entrepreneurs in Montréal. Bump helps Québec SMEs with process optimization, AI integration, custom software and cybersecurity. Engagements follow five steps: audit, analysis, proposal, implementation and maintenance.

## Workflow

Get your processes and tools working in the same direction.

Workflow automation lets systems carry out repetitive steps such as receiving requests, filing information, sending notifications, collecting approvals or synchronizing data. Bump first analyses that process with your team, then connects the appropriate tools while keeping people involved in decisions and exceptions.

## AI & Software

Software built around your reality. AI integrated where it is useful.

AI integration connects a model or agent to your data and software to perform a defined task. Bump helps SMEs select a use case, connect sources, define access and evaluate responses. Sensitive actions can remain subject to human approval.

Custom software development creates an application around business needs, such as request management, a portal, case tracking or an internal system. Bump analyses the required operation, designs the interface with users and develops the features and integrations needed to connect existing tools.

## Cybersecurity

Understand risks. Address weaknesses. Strengthen what matters.

A cybersecurity audit examines an authorized scope to identify vulnerabilities and propose prioritized corrections. Bump helps SMEs scope an audit, assess risks, secure applications and infrastructure, remediate issues and define follow-up after the engagement.

## Our method

Audit, analysis, proposal, implementation and maintenance.

A Bump engagement follows five stages: audit, analysis, proposal, implementation and maintenance. The audit establishes the current situation. Analysis prioritizes needs. The proposal defines scope and deliverables. Implementation puts the solution in place, followed by the agreed maintenance.
