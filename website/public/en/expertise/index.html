01

Start with what slows you down.

A request moves through email, a file gets copied into three tools, or information arrives too late. The starting point is usually concrete. With your team, we identify the change that simplifies the work without adding another layer of complexity.

Your situationWhere to start
Repeated tasks or manual handoffsAnalyse and automate the process
Information that is difficult to find or useAssess a focused AI integration
Existing software no longer fits your operationsScope a custom software project
Risks or access controls that need attentionDefine an authorized cybersecurity audit
02

Three divisions. One connected approach.

Workflow Optimization, AI Integration & Software Development and Cybersecurity complement one another. Software integrations need to be maintainable. Automation needs to handle exceptions. AI agents need to respect your business’s access controls and approval requirements.

  • Workflow: understand the process, simplify steps and connect tools.
  • AI and software: build or integrate systems around a business need.
  • Cybersecurity: examine risks, prioritize fixes and strengthen protection.
03

Know what you are building, and why.

Our model follows five stages: audit, analysis, proposal, implementation and maintenance. Scope, deliverables, dependencies and acceptance criteria are defined before implementation. Maintenance depends on the selected systems and the level of follow-up agreed upon.

Questions worth asking.

Do we need to know which solution to buy?+

No. You can start with an operational problem. The analysis determines whether a process improvement, integration, software project or security engagement fits the need.

Can Bump work with our existing tools?+

Yes. Integrating existing tools is part of our services. We assess their APIs, access controls and limitations before proposing a connection.
